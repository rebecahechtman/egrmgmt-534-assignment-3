01

Term premium

Daily 10-year Adrian–Crump–Moench (ACM) term premium estimates from the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, averaged by calendar month. This is a model estimate, not a directly observed market price.

New York Fed · ACM data ↗
02

Treasury duration

A constant-maturity benchmark: $100 face value, 10 years, 4% annual coupon, paid semiannually. The daily 10-year Treasury constant-maturity yield is used as yield to maturity. Modified duration is calculated daily and averaged monthly.

FRED · DGS10 ↗
03

Mortgage rates

Freddie Mac’s U.S. 30-year fixed mortgage average, via FRED. Weekly observations are averaged within each calendar month. This measures the mortgage market; it is not the return on a mortgage bond.

Calculation details & limitations +

For semiannual cash flows CFₜ = $2 (and $102 at maturity), price P = Σ CFₜ / (1 + y/2)ᵗ, for t = 1,…,20. Macaulay duration = Σ [(t/2) × present value(CFₜ)] / P. Modified duration = Macaulay duration / (1 + y/2), with y expressed as a decimal.

The benchmark resets to ten years at each date. It is not the realized duration of one aging Treasury issue, and DGS10 is a par-yield proxy. Monthly averages use available observations; missing days are not filled. The averages mix daily Treasury data with weekly mortgage observations and do not imply synchronous trades. Historical ACM estimates can be revised, and Freddie Mac changed its mortgage survey methodology in November 2022. The July–October 2023 comparison lies after that change.

Data retrieved November 9, 2026, and limited to observations dated on or before that day. Incomplete November is excluded; the chart includes complete months through October 2026. This is a saved historical dataset, not a live feed. All changes are computed from unrounded monthly averages.
